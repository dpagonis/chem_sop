# Determination of Total Acidity

## Lab Description

Course: CHEM 3000
Owner: Demetrios Pagonis

## Reagent list

- Potassium hydrogen phthalate
  - Primary standard, 100%
  - Thorn Smith unknowns
- Phenolphthalein
- Ethanol
- Sodium hydroxide

## Chemicals to prepare

- KHP unknowns
  - 6 g per student
  - In individual GHS-labelled scintillation vials
- Primary standard KHP
  - Dried at 100 C for over 1 hour
  - 5 g per student
  - In dessicator
- Phenolphthalein indicator
  - 0.05% (m/v) phenolphthalein in 1:1 (v:v) ethanol:water
  - 100 mL ethanol, 100 mL water, 0.1000 g phenolphthalein
  - 3 mL per student
  - In dropper vial
- Sodium hydroxide pellets
  - In original container
  - 5 g per student

## Laboratory setup

- At west secondary containment tray:
  - Primary standard KHP in dessicator
  - KHP unknown vials
  - Phenolphthalein indicator dropper bottle
  - Sodium hydroxide pellets
- Lab oven to 100 C

## Waste streams

- Elementary neutralization unit (excess NaOH)
